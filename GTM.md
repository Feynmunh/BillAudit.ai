Go-to-market
============

Positioning
-----------
BillAudit is an instant AI spend audit for teams buying Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and API credits without a clear renewal strategy.

Primary audience
----------------
- Startup founders
- Engineering leaders
- Finance operators
- AI platform owners

Acquisition loops
-----------------
- Shareable public audit URLs with PII stripped
- LinkedIn posts showing aggregate AI spend waste
- Product Hunt launch framed around AI budget control
- Founder-led outreach to companies visibly adopting AI coding tools

Lead magnet
-----------
Show value before asking for email. Gate the benchmark export and public share link after the savings number is visible.
